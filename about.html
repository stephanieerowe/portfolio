About me

I build practical software and keep learning as I go.

I am a freelance developer who enjoys moving between the visible parts of a product and the systems that make them work.

Background

Who I am.

I work across frontend interfaces, backend architecture, mobile apps, and AI applications. That range means I can look at a project as a whole instead of treating each feature like an isolated task.

I am especially interested in problems that need both technical care and a clear understanding of the people using the result. The best projects are not always the most complicated ones; they are the ones that make something meaningfully easier.

What I value

  • Solving real problems instead of chasing every trend.
  • Code that is readable, testable, and maintainable.
  • Understanding the "why" before jumping to implementation.
  • Systems that are capable without becoming difficult to use.
