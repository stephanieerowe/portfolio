About me

I like building useful software and learning as I go.

I'm a freelance developer who likes being involved in the whole thing: the interface people use, the code underneath it, and the small details that make it feel right.

Background

Who I am.

I work across frontend interfaces, backend architecture, mobile apps, and AI applications. That range means I can look at a project as a whole instead of treating each feature like an isolated task.

I am especially interested in problems that need technical care and a clear understanding of the people involved. I also care a lot about the people I work with; good communication, learning from each other, and building lasting professional relationships make the work meaningful to me.

What I value

  • Solving real problems instead of chasing every trend.
  • Code that is readable, testable, and maintainable.
  • Understanding the "why" before jumping to implementation.
  • Systems that are capable without becoming difficult to use.
